## Overview

**Organization settings** contains shared configuration for your organization. Available tabs and controls depend on your edition, role, and enabled features.

## When to use

Use these settings to maintain organization details, members, workflows, integrations, and the Client experience.

## Prerequisites

- Select the correct organization in Vala.
- Use an owner or admin account for administrative changes. Some settings have additional access restrictions.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the account menu and select **Organization settings**.
2. Select the tab for the setting you need. Tabs can include **Organization**, **Billing**, **Forms**, **Messaging**, **Team**, **Clients**, **Claims**, **Portal**, **Dashboard**, **Integrations**, and **AI**. Community and Partner editions show the **Organization** tab.
3. Review the current value and the effect on your team. Make the intended change using the controls shown and save it when required.
4. Reopen or review the setting and verify the affected workflow. Inform team members when their process changes.

For example, **Team** contains roles and members; **Forms** includes representative and forms workflow settings; and **Integrations** contains connected services. Settings may appear in sections within a tab. On a phone, use **On this page** to navigate those sections.

## Best practices

- Limit administrative access and change one setting at a time.
- Review the effect on existing workflows and members before changing shared configuration.

## Common mistakes

- Changing the wrong organization
- Assuming a tab or integration is available in every edition
- Failing to verify the resulting workflow

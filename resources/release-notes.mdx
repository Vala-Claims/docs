## Overview

Vala publishes customer-facing product updates in the in-app **What's New** panel. Open it to see the available update history and learn about changes.

## When to use

Check **What's New** when you want to learn about product changes or understand a changed workflow.

## Prerequisites

- A signed-in Vala staff account

## Step-by-step

1. Open the account menu and select **What's New**.
2. Review the newest updates and open related guidance where available.
3. Share relevant changes with your team and check how they affect your organization's configuration.
4. To see updates you dismissed, select **Show dismissed** in the panel.

## Best practices

- Review changes before applying them to active work.
- Check availability for your organization and role.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming every update applies to every organization
- Treating an update as a substitute for reviewing a consequential action

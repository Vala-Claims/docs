## Overview

QuickSubmit guidance is intentionally limited in this first pass. Use it only when your organization has confirmed the available workflow and required review process.

## When to use

Use QuickSubmit when your organization has approved it for the matter and you have independently reviewed every document intended for submission.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the correct organization, Client record, and matter context
- Your organization's approved submission process
- Independent review of every document intended for submission

## Step-by-step

<Note>Detailed instructions will be added after the supported workflow, permissions, and review requirements are confirmed.</Note>

## Best practices

- Verify every document before submission.
- Follow your organization's authorization and quality-control process.
- Confirm the destination and Client or matter context.

## Common mistakes

- Treating a draft or AI-generated output as submission-ready
- Using an unverified workflow
- Skipping independent review

## Overview

Team administration controls who can access your Vala organization and the work available to them.

## When to use

Use Team when onboarding, reviewing, or offboarding organization members.

## Prerequisites

- An organization owner or administrator account for invitations and member administration
- Verified member identity and business need

## Step-by-step

1. Confirm the active organization and open **Organization settings > Team**.
2. Select **Invite Member**. Enter the person's first name, last name, and verified email address.
3. Choose the appropriate **Role**. Review the displayed capabilities; if the role requires assigned Clients, select those Clients before continuing.
4. Select **Send Invitation** and check the result. A created invitation does not prove email delivery: if delivery fails, use the displayed invitation link through an approved communication channel or resolve the reported error.
5. Review existing members in **Team**. Use the role control only when available for that member. Changes save through that control; confirm the updated value and resolve any error.
6. For offboarding, review the member and organization before using the available remove control and confirming. Use **Revoke** for an outstanding invitation that should no longer be usable.

Organization roles control access. The separate **VA representative type** affects form autofill; changing one is not a substitute for changing the other. Some memberships are managed by a parent organization and cannot be edited here.

## Best practices

- Apply least-privilege access.
- Review membership on a regular schedule.
- Remove access promptly during offboarding.

## Common mistakes

- Granting broader access than needed
- Inviting the wrong email address
- Leaving former team members active

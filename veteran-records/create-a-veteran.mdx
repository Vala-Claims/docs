## Overview

A Client record is the central workspace for one veteran. It keeps the Client's information, records, communications, and claim-preparation work in context. The record opens on **Dashboard** by default.

## Choose the right tab

- **Dashboard** gives you a configurable operational view of client-specific work.
- **Claims** shows claims scoped to the Client. The same Claim records are also available from the global **Claims** workspace.
- **Details** contains Client information, organization-defined fields, notes, and related context.
- **Forms** prepares, reviews, and tracks forms and vSign work.
- **Documents** stores and organizes Client records and supporting material.
- **Messages** is the secure conversation with the Client.
- **Conditions** is a Beta, Client-level surface available only when AI is enabled for the organization.
- **Tasks** shows Client-specific follow-up work. Tasks also have a global workspace.
- **Relationships** links the reusable Contacts involved with the Client.
- **Calendar** shows Client-specific events. Calendar also has a global workspace.
- **Activity** is the Client-specific operational feed.

## Work in a Client record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find or add the Client">Open an existing Client from <strong>Contacts</strong>, or add a Client after checking for duplicates.</Step>
  <Step title="Start with the Dashboard">Review the current work, then open the tab that matches your next action.</Step>
  <Step title="Review the underlying record">When a widget, task, activity, or summary needs attention, open the related record before acting.</Step>
  <Step title="Verify before relying on information">Confirm Client information, source records, and generated or suggested content through your organization's review process.</Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Keep one clear Client record for each veteran.
- Use the global Claims, Tasks, and Calendar workspaces for cross-client work; use their Client tabs when the work is specific to one Client.
- Keep Client data and documents in the correct record before starting downstream work.

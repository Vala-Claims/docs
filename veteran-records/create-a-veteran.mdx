## Overview

A Client record keeps the records and claim-preparation work associated with one veteran together.

## When to use

Use a Client record whenever you review documents, organize conditions, prepare outputs, or track work for that veteran.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the correct organization
- Authorization to work with the veteran's information
- The identifying information required by your organization

## Step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find or add the Client">Check for an existing Client before creating a new record.</Step>
  <Step title="Verify the profile">Confirm identifying information against an approved source.</Step>
  <Step title="Open the needed tab">Continue to Claims, Details, Forms, Documents, Messages, Conditions, Tasks, Relationships, Calendar, or Activity.</Step>
  <Step title="Review before leaving">Make sure new information and outputs belong to the correct Client record.</Step>
</Steps>

## Client record tabs

The Client record opens on <strong>Dashboard</strong>. Use the tabs to work in the right context:

- <strong>Claims</strong> for the Client's claim work.
- <strong>Details</strong> for Client information.
- <strong>Forms</strong>, <strong>Documents</strong>, and <strong>Messages</strong> for related workflows.
- <strong>Conditions</strong> for condition-level work. This tab is Beta and appears only when AI is enabled for the organization.
- <strong>Tasks</strong>, <strong>Relationships</strong>, <strong>Calendar</strong>, and <strong>Activity</strong> for ongoing work and context.

## Best practices

- Keep one clear Client record for each veteran.
- Verify extracted or generated information against sources.
- Follow consistent naming and review conventions.

## Common mistakes

- Creating duplicate Client records
- Adding one veteran's records to another Client
- Relying on unverified extracted information

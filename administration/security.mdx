## Overview

Protecting veteran and organization information is a shared responsibility. Follow your organization's security and privacy procedures when using Vala.

## When to use

Use this guidance when onboarding, reviewing access, sharing information, or responding to a suspected account issue.

## Prerequisites

- Your organization's security and privacy procedures
- Access to the correct account and organization

## Step-by-step

1. Sign in with your own account. Do not share credentials.
2. Confirm the organization and Client before viewing, uploading, or sharing information.
3. Ask an owner or admin to review team roles and remove access that is no longer needed. Review connected integrations and API key access with your administrator.
4. Lock or sign out of unattended devices. Report suspected account or data issues promptly through your organization's established process and contact Vala support when appropriate.

## Best practices

- Grant only the access needed for each role.
- Check recipients and attachments before sending sensitive information.
- Follow your organization's approved incident reporting process.

## Common mistakes

- Sharing login credentials or API keys
- Uploading information to the wrong Client
- Delaying a report of suspicious activity

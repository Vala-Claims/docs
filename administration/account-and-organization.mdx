## Overview

Account and organization administration covers settings that apply to you and settings that may affect your Vala organization. Access depends on your role and permissions.

## When to use

Use this page when you need to find account settings, Profile, Notifications, Vala Network, or organization-wide administrative controls.

## Prerequisites

- An active Vala account
- The required account or administrative permission

## Step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the scope">Decide whether the change applies to your account or to the organization.</Step>
  <Step title="Open the appropriate settings">Use <strong>Account settings</strong> for account-level preferences and <strong>Organization settings</strong> for organization-level configuration available to your role.</Step>
  <Step title="Review before changing">Confirm the organization, people, and workflows that may be affected.</Step>
  <Step title="Verify the result">Check that the intended setting changed without affecting unrelated work.</Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Use the least access needed for your role.
- Treat organization changes as shared changes.
- Contact an administrator or support when the required control is unavailable.

## Common mistakes

- Changing an organization setting when an account preference is intended
- Assuming all team members can access billing or security controls
- Making a shared change without confirming its scope

## Overview

Billing covers the subscription and payment information associated with your Vala organization.

## When to use

Use this page when reviewing your plan, payment details, invoices, or a billing question.

## Prerequisites

- An organization owner or administrator account with access to **Billing**
- The correct Vala organization

## Step-by-step

1. Confirm the active organization and open **Organization settings > Billing**.
2. Review **Current plan**, its subscription status, and the current period when shown. If included Client usage is displayed, select the used count to inspect that period's list.
3. To update payment details, select **Update payment** and follow the secure Stripe page. Return to Vala and check the result. Do not send full payment credentials by email.
4. If **Change plan** is available, choose the desired plan and read **Confirm plan change**, including the displayed charge or credit and effective timing. Select **Cancel** to leave the plan unchanged, or **Confirm change** only when authorized to apply it.
5. Contact support@valaclaims.com for cancellation, Enterprise plans, unavailable self-service controls, or a billing question. Include the relevant invoice or period, without sensitive payment credentials.

If **Billing request failed** appears, use **Retry** to reload the summary. Check the current plan before repeating a plan change whose outcome is uncertain.

## Best practices

- Limit billing access to authorized team members.
- Keep billing contact information current.
- Reference invoice details when asking for help.

## Common mistakes

- Reviewing billing in the wrong organization
- Sharing full payment credentials by email
- Assuming every team role can view billing

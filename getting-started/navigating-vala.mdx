## Overview

Vala's primary navigation organizes daily work into workspaces. The areas available to you can vary by your role, organization settings, and enabled features.

<Note>
Community Edition has a smaller navigation: <strong>Documents</strong>, <strong>Vala Network</strong>, and <strong>Backpay Calculator</strong>. The rest of this guide describes the full Vala product.
</Note>

## When to use

Use this page when you are new to Vala, return to a workflow after a change, or need to find where an action belongs.

## Prerequisites

- An active Vala account
- Access to the correct organization

## Step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the global navigation">Open <strong>Ask Vala</strong>, <strong>Dashboard</strong>, <strong>Contacts</strong>, <strong>Claims</strong>, <strong>Tasks</strong>, <strong>Notes</strong>, or <strong>Calendar</strong> for daily work.</Step>
  <Step title="Use workspace views">In Contacts, Claims, Templates, and Workflows, use the workspace views to narrow to the work you need.</Step>
  <Step title="Open a Client record">In <strong>Contacts</strong>, <strong>Clients</strong> is the default view. Open the Client record to work with client-specific information and tasks.</Step>
  <Step title="Use supporting workspaces">Open <strong>Templates</strong>, <strong>Workflows</strong>, <strong>Calls</strong>, or <strong>Calculators</strong> when those match your task.</Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Confirm the active organization and Client record before adding or reviewing information.
- Use the interface label shown in Vala when searching the Help Center.
- Check whether a feature is available to your role before assuming it is missing.

## Common mistakes

- Treating a view in Contacts or Claims as a separate record type
- Looking for older labels such as Library, Web Forms, or Automations
- Working in the wrong Client record

## Overview

Templates is the Vala workspace for reusable materials. Its views are <strong>Documents</strong>, <strong>Questionnaires</strong>, <strong>Reports</strong>, and <strong>Messages</strong>.

## When to use

Use Templates when you need to find or manage a reusable item for an approved workflow.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the correct organization
- The permissions required for the template or view you need

## Step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates">Select <strong>Templates</strong> from the global navigation.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the right view">Use <strong>Documents</strong>, <strong>Questionnaires</strong>, <strong>Reports</strong>, or <strong>Messages</strong> based on the reusable item you need.</Step>
  <Step title="Verify before use">Review the item and follow your organization's approval process before using it in a Client workflow.</Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Choose the reusable item that matches the approved workflow.
- Verify Client-specific information before using a reusable item.
- Keep reusable report templates distinct from generated report outputs.

## Common mistakes

- Looking for older labels such as Library, Web Forms, or Message Templates
- Treating a reusable report template as a generated report
- Using a template without confirming the organization and workflow

## Overview

The Vala QuickSubmit browser add-on helps you hand off a reviewed PDF or eligible signing packet to VA QuickSubmit. You complete and submit the filing in QuickSubmit. Vala's recorded completion is staff-reported and does not confirm VA receipt.

## When to use

Use this handoff when your organization has authorized the filing and you have reviewed the documents and recipient information.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the correct Client and permission to submit to VA. Owners and admins have this permission; other roles need **Submit to VA** access.
- An authorized VA QuickSubmit account and the [Vala QuickSubmit Chrome add-on](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/vala-quicksubmit/fajgcpgllmihnmpfdgphahgkpndelmea).
- Reviewed, complete documents and your organization's required authorization.

## Step-by-step

1. Review the Client, document contents, signatures, and supporting evidence.
2. From a form's actions choose **Submit to VA**. For a completed client-linked signing packet, choose **Submit packet to VA**, review the included files and order, and select separate files or a combined PDF. From **Documents**, select one eligible PDF and choose **Submit to VA**.
3. Continue in VA QuickSubmit. Review the attached files and all fields, complete the VA workflow, and submit there. Resolve any missing-field or transfer messages before submitting.
4. After you submit in QuickSubmit, use **I submitted this in QuickSubmit** in the Vala handoff panel. If you cancel, use **Cancel this handoff**. Check the resulting status in Vala and retain VA confirmation according to your organization's process.

For an unfinished handoff, review its status and use **Retry QuickSubmit** where offered. Before retrying, confirm whether a submission already occurred to avoid a duplicate.

## Best practices

- Verify the correct Client and every file before submission.
- A standalone Documents handoff accepts one eligible PDF. Use a signing packet when sending multiple files through the packet flow.
- Treat the staff-reported status as a record of your confirmation, not a VA receipt.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming signing, downloading, or attaching a file submits it to VA
- Retrying without checking whether the original submission was completed
- Treating a draft or AI-generated output as ready without review

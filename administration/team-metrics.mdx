## Overview

**Team Metrics** shows recorded activity and workload for the selected period and scope. Open it through **Coaching detail** in the account menu. The separate **Team metrics** or **Office metrics** entry opens a broader results view.

## When to use

Review workload and recorded team activity, then identify questions for a coaching conversation or operational follow-up.

## Prerequisites

- **Team Metrics** permission in the correct organization
- Owner/admin access or a permission grant from your organization administrator
- A home-office assignment when your office-scoped access requires one

## Step-by-step

1. Open the account menu and select **Coaching detail** to reach **Team Metrics**.
2. Check the displayed period. The default is the last 30 days. Choose a day preset or enter **Start** and **End**, then select **Apply**.
3. Read the fiscal-year caption before using **YTD**, **This fiscal year**, or **Last fiscal year**. Fiscal presets are unavailable when selected offices do not share a fiscal year.
4. Review the member and claim-status filters. If office controls are available, confirm **Client office** and **Office** before comparing figures. Other office-scoped viewers stay within their assigned office.
5. Review the summary cards, activity chart, and per-member breakdown. Use **Case Ownership** and **Needs Attention** for follow-up context.
6. Select **Download CSV** when you need the worksheet for the applied period and scope. Handle the export under your organization's information-sharing rules.

## Best practices

- Compare figures only after checking the same period, filters, and office basis.
- Discuss the context behind activity counts before drawing performance conclusions.
- Distinguish recorded results from modeled estimates in additional results sections.

## Common mistakes

- Opening **Team metrics** and expecting the **Coaching detail** page
- Treating a fiscal-year preset as a calendar-year preset
- Assuming office-scoped access includes the whole organization
- Treating activity totals alone as evidence of service quality

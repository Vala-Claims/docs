## Overview

**Activity** is the Client-specific operational feed. It brings together relevant updates so you can see what changed, what is new, and where to continue the work.

![The Activity tab showing document, message, task, claim, and calendar updates for one Client.](/images/client-record/activity.png)

*Unread items are marked so you can prioritize new Client-specific activity.*

## Review activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Activity">Open the Client record and select <strong>Activity</strong>.</Step>
  <Step title="Filter the feed">Choose <strong>All Activities</strong> or narrow the list to <strong>Tasks</strong>, <strong>Messages</strong>, <strong>Documents</strong>, <strong>Client Updates</strong>, <strong>Claims</strong>, <strong>Calls</strong>, <strong>Calendar</strong>, <strong>Deadlines</strong>, or <strong>System</strong>.</Step>
  <Step title="Open the related work">Select an activity item when a related destination is available. New items are marked until they are read.</Step>
  <Step title="Refresh or page through results">Use refresh when you need the latest activity and adjust rows per page or pagination when the feed is longer.</Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Use the filter to focus on the type of change you need to review.
- Open the linked Client, Claim, Document, message, or task before acting on an activity entry.
- Treat Activity as context for the work, not as a replacement for reviewing the underlying record.

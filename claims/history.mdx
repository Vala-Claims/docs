## Overview

The **History** tab records Claim changes so your team can review what changed and when. Each entry can show the change, previous value, new value, person, and timestamp. Some entries are marked **Audit** and others **Activity** when that context is available in the record.

![The Claim History tab showing status, document, assignment, form, and Condition changes.](/images/claims/history.png)

## Filter the history

Use the filter to show **All changes** or narrow the list to **Status**, **Review lane**, **Category**, **Stage**, **Tags**, **Assignee**, **Custom fields**, **Dates**, **Documents**, **Forms**, **Tasks**, or **Conditions**. Use refresh when you need the latest recorded entries, and page through the list when more entries are available.

## History and comments serve different purposes

Use History to review recorded Claim changes. Use the **Comments** section in [Claim Details](/claims/details) for internal discussion and working context. Comments do not replace the change record, and Client messages belong in the Client's Messages workspace.

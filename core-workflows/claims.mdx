## Overview

Claims tracks VA claim matters associated with Clients. The global Claims workspace includes <strong>All Claims</strong>, <strong>Open</strong>, <strong>Assigned to me</strong>, <strong>Ready to Review</strong>, <strong>Pending Evidence</strong>, <strong>Pending Decision</strong>, <strong>Submitted</strong>, and <strong>Closed</strong> views.

## When to use

Use Claims when your organization needs to organize a VA claim matter and review related work.

## Prerequisites

- The correct Client record
- Your organization's direction on the claim being prepared

## Claim record tabs

Use <strong>Details</strong>, <strong>Dates</strong>, and <strong>History</strong> to review a Claim record. <strong>Financials</strong> appears only for viewers with permission.

## From a Client record

The **Claims** tab in a Client record is scoped to that Client. Search the Client's claims, select **Add Claim** when you need to create one, then open the Claim record for its details. The underlying Claim is the same record available from the global **Claims** workspace, so use the Client tab for client-specific context and the global workspace for work across Clients.

## Best practices

- Keep the scope clear and consistent.
- Separate verified facts from working notes or AI output.
- Use your organization's review process before submission.

## Common mistakes

- Working in the wrong Claim or Client context
- Treating a draft output as submission-ready
- Leaving related work disconnected or unclear

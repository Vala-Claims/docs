## Overview

Use the existing **Activity**, **History**, and **Dates** views to review a matter chronologically. This page is a guide to those views, not instructions for a separate Timeline workspace.

## When to use

Use chronological review when sequence, timing, or changes over time matter to your work.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the correct Client and Claim records
- Relevant records available for review

## Step-by-step

1. Open the Client record and select **Activity** to review operational updates for that Client. Narrow the feed by the type of activity you need.
2. Open the relevant Claim and select **History** to review recorded changes, including previous and new values when available. Filter the history to the field group you need.
3. Use the Claim's **Dates** tab to review its recorded dates. Verify consequential dates against the underlying records.
4. Follow the relevant entry to its source or related work when a destination is available. Refresh or page through the view to review more entries.

## Best practices

- Review surrounding events, not only isolated entries.
- Distinguish record dates from dates mentioned within a record.
- Verify consequential dates independently.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming every event is captured
- Confusing document date with event date
- Relying on an activity or history entry without source review

## Overview

Create a Client record to begin organized claim-preparation work for a veteran. Start in **Contacts**, confirm that the Client does not already exist, then add the information presented in the Client form.

![The Add Client dialog in the Clients view.](/images/veteran-intake/add-client-dialog.png)

*Add the information shown in the form, then open the new Client record to continue the workflow.*

## Before you begin

- Confirm you are working in the correct organization.
- Gather only the information your organization is authorized to process.
- Check for an existing Client to avoid creating a duplicate.

## Add a Client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">Select <strong>Contacts</strong> in the global navigation.</Step>
  <Step title="Use Clients">Select <strong>Clients</strong>, which is the default Contacts view.</Step>
  <Step title="Check first">Search for the veteran and review possible matches before creating a new record.</Step>
  <Step title="Select Add Client">Select <strong>Add Client</strong>.</Step>
  <Step title="Complete the form">Enter the Client information currently presented. First and last name are marked as required; complete other fields as appropriate for the Client and your organization.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Select <strong>Add Client</strong> to save the record.</Step>
  <Step title="Continue in the Client record">Open the Client record and continue with the appropriate workflow, often <strong>Documents</strong> or other intake work.</Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Verify names and contact information against an approved source.
- Add information incrementally if your organization does not yet have every detail.
- Review the Client record after saving to make sure you are in the correct matter.

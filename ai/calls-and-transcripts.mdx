## Overview

**Calls** brings your call history and available transcripts into one workspace. Select a call to review its date, duration, direction, and associated Client before using its information.

## When to use

Use it when an authorized call or transcript is relevant to your team's work.

## Prerequisites

- Access to **Calls** in your organization
- Required participant notice, consent, and authorization
- The correct Client or matter context

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Calls** from the navigation. Use **Calls** to browse call history or **Clients** to find calls associated with a Client.
2. Select the call you need. Check the Client or phone number and the call date before reviewing its contents.
3. Read **Transcript** when one is available. **No transcript for this call** means there is no transcript to review; **Transcription unavailable** indicates a failed transcription. Do not treat either message as a completed transcript.
4. Review any **Extracted data** against the conversation and source records. If **Apply to client** is available, open it, correct the proposed values in **Edit & apply to client**, and review them before applying the change.
5. To make an authorized outbound call, use **Keypad** or **Call again**. Verify the destination and required notice or consent before selecting **Start call**. Use **End call** when finished.

<Note>
  Transcripts and extracted information depend on what is available for that call. This guide does not promise a transcript for every call or a general file-upload workflow.
</Note>

## Best practices

- Follow consent and privacy requirements.
- Treat automated transcription as a draft.
- Review important names, dates, and statements carefully.

## Common mistakes

- Recording or processing a call without required authorization
- Assigning a transcript to the wrong matter
- Treating transcript text as perfectly accurate

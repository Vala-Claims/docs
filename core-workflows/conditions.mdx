## Overview

The <strong>Conditions</strong> tab provides a focused way to organize relevant information on a Client record. The <strong>Conditions</strong> tab is Beta and is available only when AI is enabled for the organization.

## When to use

Use Conditions when your team is reviewing condition-specific evidence and preparing related work.

## Prerequisites

- The correct Client record
- Reviewed source records
- Your organization's direction on the matter

Claim context can be relevant when a condition is involved in active claim work, but it is not required to use Conditions.

## Best practices

- Keep condition names and scope consistent.
- Connect findings to reviewable source material.
- Record uncertainty instead of presenting it as fact.

## Common mistakes

- Duplicating the same condition
- Mixing information from unrelated Client matters
- Treating an AI suggestion as a verified conclusion

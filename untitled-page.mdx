## Overview

The **Pension & A&A Calculator** is a worksheet for estimated pension scenarios, including Housebound and Aid & Attendance inputs. It does not establish eligibility or provide an official benefit determination.

## When to use

Organize a proposed scenario and identify inputs or assumptions that need professional review.

## Prerequisites

- Access to **Resources > Pension & A&A Calculator**
- Verified applicant, dependent, income, expense, asset, and transfer information
- Professional review of eligibility assumptions and any result before use

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Resources > Pension & A&A Calculator**. The page loads an example and its result; replace the example before using it for your own scenario.
2. Set **Proposed Application Date**, **Applicant Type**, and **Dependents (spouse/children)**. Review the wartime-service, Housebound, and Aid & Attendance confirmations rather than leaving example assumptions in place.
3. Choose the benefit tier and enter the annual income and medical or care expenses in the labeled fields. Check that you are entering annual amounts.
4. Enter **Countable Assets** and any transfers or gifts for review. The worksheet does not decide which assets or expenses should legally count.
5. Select **Calculate** after entering or changing values.
6. Review the estimated pension, net-worth information, forms checklist, and **Warnings / Assumptions**. Have a qualified representative verify the facts and applicable requirements before acting.
7. Use **Clear Data** for a blank worksheet or **Reset to Example** to restore the sample. Neither action validates a real applicant's information.

## Best practices

- Keep supporting information available for professional review.
- Check annual amounts and all confirmations before recalculating.
- Treat the forms checklist as a review aid rather than a complete filing instruction.

## Common mistakes

- Treating the initial example result as an eligibility decision
- Entering monthly amounts into annual income or expense fields
- Changing inputs without selecting **Calculate** again
- Treating a warning-free result as approval or legal advice

## Overview

Workflows run ordered **Then** actions when a **When** event matches your filters. The [trigger and action catalog](/core-workflows/workflow-catalog) lists the available types and their integration identifiers.

## When to use

Use a workflow for a repeatable process such as creating a follow-up task or notifying staff when a document is ready.

## Prerequisites

- Workflows are available in the full CRM, not Community or Partner Edition.
- Access depends on your role, organization settings, workflow locks, and office scope. Ask an owner or admin if you cannot manage a workflow.
- Prepare any templates, tags, checklists, or other resources the actions need.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Workflows** and choose **New Workflow**, then **Builder**. Alternatively, open **Recipes** and choose **Use recipe**.
2. Choose a **When** trigger and narrow it with filters where needed. Add and configure the **Then** actions in order.
3. Review recipients, linked resources, and the **Active** setting before saving. Turn **Active** off if the workflow should remain a draft. Recipes can start with **Active** on.
4. Review definitions in **Active** or **Draft**. Select a card to preview it, or use its edit control to change it.
5. After activation, check **History** for execution results and errors. Deactivate the workflow if it needs correction.

The AI Workflow Builder, when available, creates a draft for your review. Review every trigger, filter, action, and resource before saving or activating it.

## Best practices

- Start with a narrow scope and verify results before expanding it.
- Avoid overlapping workflows that send duplicate notifications.
- Review consequential outputs and keep organization-specific resources current.

## Common mistakes

- Saving a recipe as active before reviewing its configuration
- Choosing a trigger without the Client or Claim context an action needs
- Assuming a workflow ran successfully without checking **History**

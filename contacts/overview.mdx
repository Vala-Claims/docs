## Overview

Contacts organizes people and organizations used in your Vala work. <strong>Clients</strong> is the default Contacts view.

## When to use

Use Contacts to find or add a Client, or to work with related representatives, providers, organizations, and other contacts.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the correct organization
- Authorization to work with the contact information involved

## Step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">Select <strong>Contacts</strong> from the global navigation.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose a view">Use <strong>Clients</strong>, <strong>Representatives</strong>, <strong>Providers</strong>, <strong>Organizations</strong>, or <strong>All Contacts</strong> to find the right entry point.</Step>
  <Step title="Open the record">Open the Client or contact record and verify its identity before making changes.</Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- Check for an existing entry before adding another one.
- Use Client for the Vala interface record and veteran for the person represented.
- Confirm relationships before relying on a contact in a Client workflow.

## Common mistakes

- Creating a duplicate Client or contact
- Selecting the wrong contact view
- Changing information without confirming the organization and record

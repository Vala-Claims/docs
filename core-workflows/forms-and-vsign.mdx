## Overview

Use **Forms** in a Client record to prepare supported VA forms and eligible reusable organization documents, review the information shown, and collect electronic signatures through vSign. Available forms and sending options can vary by organization configuration.

![The Forms tab with Draft, In Review, Sent for Signature, and Signed columns.](/images/client-record/forms.png)

*The Forms board tracks a document from preparation through signing.*

## Before you begin

- Open the correct Client record.
- Verify the Client information, form fields, and intended recipients.
- Confirm that the form or reusable document is available to your organization.

## Prepare and send a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add or generate the document">Use the available form or eligible reusable organization document option.</Step>
  <Step title="Review populated information">Open the document, review the populated values, and edit where the form supports editing.</Step>
  <Step title="Move through review">Use the current board columns: <strong>Draft</strong>, <strong>In Review</strong>, <strong>Sent for Signature</strong>, and <strong>Signed</strong>.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm recipients">Before sending, verify every form field and recipient. Review any visible warning about unpopulated form fields.</Step>
  <Step title="Send and monitor">Send for signature, follow the signing status, and review the signed result before relying on it.</Step>
</Steps>

## Signing choices

You can send a single document for signature or create a multi-document signing packet when at least two eligible documents are available. A saved **Primary POC** can be used where supported. For each send, you can choose or override the POC without changing the saved relationship. Choose **Client (self)** when the Client should sign directly.

## Best practices

- Verify every form value and recipient against approved information before sending.
- Use the Primary POC only when it is the appropriate contact for that send.
- Review the signed result and preserve it according to your organization's process.

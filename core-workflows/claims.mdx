## Overview

Claims bring together the work needed for a particular VA disability claim or claim-preparation effort. The Claims workspace includes <strong>All Claims</strong>, <strong>Open</strong>, <strong>Assigned to me</strong>, <strong>Ready to Review</strong>, <strong>Pending Evidence</strong>, <strong>Pending Decision</strong>, <strong>Submitted</strong>, and <strong>Closed</strong> views.

## When to use

Use Claims when your organization needs to organize related conditions, evidence, reports, forms, and tasks.

## Prerequisites

- The correct Client record
- Your organization's direction on the claim being prepared

## Claim record tabs

Use <strong>Details</strong>, <strong>Dates</strong>, and <strong>History</strong> to review a Claim record. <strong>Financials</strong> appears only for viewers with permission.

## Best practices

- Keep the scope clear and consistent.
- Separate verified facts from working notes or AI output.
- Use your organization's review process before submission.

## Common mistakes

- Working in the wrong Client record
- Treating a draft output as submission-ready
- Leaving related work disconnected or unclear

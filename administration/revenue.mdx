## Overview

**Revenue** summarizes recorded claim financials, including awards, fees, and payment status. The page states whether it is showing your caseload or the whole organization.

## When to use

Review recorded claim financials for a reporting period or export those records for reconciliation.

## Prerequisites

- **Claim Financials** permission in the correct organization
- Owner/admin access or a permission grant from your organization administrator
- Financial information recorded on the relevant claims

## Step-by-step

1. Open the account menu and select **Revenue**.
2. Read **Showing your caseload** or **Showing whole organization** before interpreting the totals. Your access determines the reporting scope.
3. Choose **This Month**, **Year to Date**, **All Time**, or **Custom**. The reporting period uses claim decision dates.
4. For **Custom**, enter a **Start date**, an **End date**, or both, then select **Apply**. If you enter both, the start must be on or before the end. No custom report is shown until a range is applied.
5. Review the summary cards, chart, and claim-type breakdowns for that scope and period. Recorded benefits and fees are different measures; review each label.
6. Select **Export CSV** to download claim financials for the same applied period and authorized scope. Review the export before sharing it.

## Best practices

- Reconcile totals with the underlying recorded claim financials and payment status.
- Check the scope caption and applied dates before comparing periods or exports.
- Handle exported information under your organization's access and sharing rules.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming **Claim Financials** access always means organization-wide reporting
- Expecting a custom range to apply before selecting **Apply**
- Treating recorded fees earned as proof that payment was received
- Treating this dashboard or a calculator estimate as accounting, legal, or financial advice
